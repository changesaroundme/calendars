Purpose

TxDOT is proposing improvements along 13 miles of I-35 from SH 29 to SH 45 North in Williamson County, Texas.
Description

The I-35 Georgetown to Round Rock Project proposes to:

  • Add two non-tolled high-occupancy vehicle managed lanes in each direction.
  • Add one mainlane in each direction between SH 29 and RM 1431.
  • Relocate and modify entrance and exit ramps.
  • Reconstruct several bridges and cross street intersections.
  • Add bypass lanes where needed.
  • Add shared-use paths throughout the corridor.

Any environmental documentation, studies, maps or drawings showing the project location and design, tentative construction schedules and other information regarding the proposed project are on file and available for inspection Monday through Friday between the hours of 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. at 7901 N. I-35, Austin, TX 78753 or by calling 512-939-3953. These materials will also be available in hard copy form for review at the in-person meeting and online on this webpage.

US 79 from I-35 to east of FM 1460 - virtual public meeting

Public comment deadline All comments must be received on or before Wednesday, Sept. 16, 2026, to be included in the official record of the public meeting.
Virtual details The public meeting materials, including a pre-recorded video presentation, will be posted on this webpage on Tuesday, Sept. 1, 2026 by 5:30 p.m.
Purpose

TxDOT and the city of Round Rock are proposing improvements to US 79 from I-35 to east of A.W. Grimes Boulevard in Williamson County, Texas. This notice advises the public that TxDOT will be hosting a virtual public meeting.
Description

The proposed improvements include widening the existing US 79 highway to add a third travel lane in each direction with a raised median and selected median breaks, constructing intersection improvements and adding shared-use paths.
How to make a comment

Comments must be received or postmarked by Wednesday, Sept. 16, 2026, to be included in the official record of the public meeting.

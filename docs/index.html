Central Texas Public Meeting Calendars

A Changes Around Me project.

Subscribable calendars of Central Texas public meetings, rebuilt daily from official sources. "Subscribe" adds a live feed to your calendar app; "Download" grabs a one-time snapshot.

Tip: in Apple Calendar, set the subscription's Auto-refresh to "Every day" (the default is weekly) so schedule changes reach you promptly. Google Calendar refreshes on its own schedule, roughly daily.

📅 Browse the combined calendar — month and list views, filterable by organization.

Everything (combined)

All organizations below, in one feed.

CAMPO

Capital Area MPO — Transportation Policy Board and Technical Advisory Committee. Mirrored from CAMPO's own published feed.

CapMetro

Board of Directors and committees, from CapMetro's Legistar calendar and API.

TTC (Texas Transportation Commission)

The commission overseeing TxDOT, from its published schedule — dates post a year ahead; agenda links appear ~8 days out.

LCRA

LCRA Board, committees, and Transmission Services Corp. — all-day entries from their annual schedule; exact times post with agendas.

CTRMA

Central Texas Regional Mobility Authority board meetings, from their upcoming-meetings page.

City of Austin

City Council and committees (timed, with agendas, ~weeks ahead) plus tracked boards & commissions (full-year dates; times post with agendas).

ATP

Austin Transit Partnership — Board and Community Advisory Committee, mirrored from ATP's own published feed.

TxDOT Events

TxDOT beyond the commission: public involvement events and comment windows from planning pages (currently the UTP), plus advisory-committee meetings (Bicycle & Pedestrian, Public Transportation).

A Changes Around Me project (source), built with heavy AI assistance, aggregating official public postings on a best-effort basis. Entries can be wrong, stale, or missing — always confirm details with the linked source before planning around them, and don't rely on these calendars for anything safety-critical, legal-deadline, or financial.
